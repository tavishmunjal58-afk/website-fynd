Final requested duration: 4 minutes 30 seconds. 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 video with AAC audio.
The previously rendered native-resolution HTML/CSS scenes are retimed to eighteen seconds each. Intermediate motion frames are blended at 30 fps; no spatial upscaling is used. This is a production compromise, not a fresh 4K render.
British female narration is newly written and generated for this cut at approximately 140 words per minute. Each scene begins speech at +0.6 seconds and finishes before its outgoing transition.

## Production disclosure

Visuals: real app DOM and CSS, isolated in a cinematic HTML stage; Chromium/Playwright renders, FFmpeg editing and delivery. Original UI is light, surrounded by a dark stage. Existing C monogram and indigo accent retained.
Source application: ramyakusunuri/Creator-Campaign-Contribution-Planner, commit c0a2838e208ae224bc861310606327886663f912.
All displayed creator and financial data is illustrative. Finder examples use the repository’s sample handles and figures in a browser fixture, not live API results. No fabricated customers, testimonials or performance claims.
Voice: local Piper en-gb-southern_english_female-low, 16 kHz source audio. Voice quality is substantially below premium cloud neural or recorded studio narration.
Voice model source: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/releases/tag/v0.0.2. Source dataset: OpenSLR 83, CC BY-SA 4.0. See included model card. Narration is synthetic.
Music and effects: original generated ambient harmonies, piano-like tones, soft bass, shaped noise transitions, UI ticks and impacts. No licensed commercial music or stock recordings.
Mix: speech high-pass at 70 Hz, light compression, de-essing and short echo reverb approximation. Music ducked 8 dB during the speech intervals. Final loudness and peak checks are listed in quality-report.md.
Limitations: 1080p rather than 4K; perspective and lighting are CSS approximations, not physical 3D or volumetric rendering; restrained generated sound rather than a studio orchestra; caption sentence timing is estimated within each exactly timed voice segment; retimed visuals use frame blending. No claim of Apple/Rolex production equivalence.
Verification: automated checks cover every decoded video frame; representative full-resolution stills and transition frames are reviewed visually. This is not manual inspection of all 8,100 frames.
