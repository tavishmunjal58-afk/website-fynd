## Audio and source disclosure

Voice: Piper en-gb-southern_english_female-low, 16 kHz source model; slowed to approximately 140 words per minute. It cannot match premium studio narration.
Music: original synthesised warm pads, sparse bell/piano-like partials, sub-bass, and shaped noise. Effects: original filtered noise sweeps, short glass-like transients, soft low-frequency impacts. No stock or commercial recordings.
Model source: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/releases/tag/v0.0.2. Source voice dataset: OpenSLR 83, CC BY-SA 4.0; model card supplied.
Finder result examples reuse the repository’s synthetic persona handles and figures in an isolated browser fixture. They are not live API results. API credentials are not configured.
The film preserves the existing C monogram; no new logo or commercial performance claim is introduced.

## Production limits

CSS perspective, blur, gradients and compositing approximate reflections, depth and lighting; no physically rendered 3D or real volumetric light. Native 1080p selected after 4K capture benchmark was about six times slower. No claim of Apple/Rolex production equivalence.
Estimated-profit results are illustrative. The aggregate planner does not adjust for returns, incrementality, attribution overlap, or all omitted costs. Profile sample return handling is a separate calculation.
Caption file contains complete voice lines; captions are optional in playback rather than permanently covering the UI.
Final quality report distinguishes automated full-frame decoding from sampled visual inspection. No claim of manual inspection of every frame.
