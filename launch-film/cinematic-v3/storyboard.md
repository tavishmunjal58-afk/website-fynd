# Creator Campaign Contribution Planner — dark cinematic film

Storyboard for the revised 4:22 master. Landscape 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps. The opening lasts 10 seconds; each of the fourteen following scenes lasts 18 seconds.

## Visual and timing treatment

The interface uses real implemented app structures with a dark presentation treatment. A graphite background, restrained indigo accents, fine curved light lines, and eased camera moves fill the frame. Feature headings recede after approximately eight seconds to leave more room for the interface. Persistent footer text identifies the dark-mode visualisation and illustrative data; discovery scenes also state that API setup is required.

Scene boundaries use a 1.6-second eased combination of horizontal movement, slight scale, blur, opacity, and a soft light veil. This is the transition actually rendered; the film does not contain bespoke 3D object morphs. Curved cursor moves, click ripples, staggered table rows, selected value counts, and coupon typing are animated where applicable.

Narration starts 0.6 seconds into each scene and finishes before its outgoing transition begins. Times below reflect the generated narration, including sentence pauses. The opening was newly rendered with a smaller 72 px headline and no small tagline. The remaining native-resolution feature footage keeps its original timing and appears eight seconds earlier.

## Production notes and limits

- **Rendering:** 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 16:9; duration 4:22. The opening was rendered again from HTML/CSS with a smaller 72 px headline and a 10-second duration. Its small tagline was removed. The fourteen remaining 18-second scenes retain the native 1080p feature footage, shifted eight seconds earlier. Chromium/Playwright and FFmpeg were used. The master is 1080p rather than 4K; the existing feature footage was not slowed or interpolated.
- **Narration:** Kokoro v1.0 local neural TTS, British male voice `bm_george`, generated at 24 kHz and processed for the 48 kHz mix. This is not ElevenLabs or a human studio performance. Delivery and pronunciation remain subject to local TTS limitations.
- **Music and effects:** Original, procedurally synthesized ambient pads, sparse bell/piano-like tones, low impacts, filtered transition noise, UI ticks, and quiet room-like texture. Seven additional, self-generated keyboard clicks follow the seven visible letters typed into the coupon field, quantized to their rendered 30 fps frames. No keyboard sounds were added to fields that do not visibly type. No stock recordings or third-party music samples were used. Music is ducked by 8 dB during speech. The mix targets approximately −14 LUFS with true peak below −1 dBTP; measured delivery values are reported separately.
- **Interface accuracy:** Layouts and feature labels come from the implemented app. The dark palette is a film visualisation; it does not establish that the shipped application already has a dark-mode feature. The C monogram is a film graphic treatment. Demonstration figures, creator records, customer orders, and discovery results are illustrative.
- **Product limitations shown or spoken:** Campaign estimates do not yet account for returns or attribution overlap. Profile statistics describe a separate sample. Coupon edits are local to the browser and do not create live merchant coupons. Discovery requires configured server/account access and was not verified against live APIs. Instagram discovery looks up known business/creator accounts. Browser shortlist entries expire after thirty days.
- **Reference and quality:** The supplied YouTube reference could not be inspected because access was blocked. This is a programmatically animated product film, not a bespoke studio 3D production, and it should not be represented as matching Apple or Rolex production quality shot for shot.
- **Subtitles:** Removed as requested. The final MP4 has no subtitle track, and no SRT is included in this revision. Product headings, interface labels, and the illustrative-data disclosure remain part of the picture.
